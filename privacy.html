Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vespera Notes ("we", "our", or "the application"), developed by Vespera Labs, handles your data.

1. Offline-First Architecture

Vespera Notes is built with an offline-first architecture. All your notes, categories, tags, and settings are stored locally on your device storage by default. We do not operate proprietary servers to read, process, or sell your personal notes.

2. Google Drive Synchronization

If you choose to enable cloud synchronization, Vespera Notes connects directly with Google Drive using the official Google Identity and Google Drive APIs.

  • Requested Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata
  • Dedicated App Data: The application requests access solely to its own hidden Application Data Folder (appDataFolder).
  • No Access to Personal Drive Files: The application cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your documents, photos, or existing files stored in your personal Google Drive.
  • Sync Payload: The application only writes and reads its own encrypted or formatted sync payload file (vespera_notes_sync.json) inside the dedicated app folder.

3. Data Collection and Telemetry

We do not collect personal analytics, location data, biometric data, or telemetry. Authentication tokens obtained via Google Identity are held strictly on your device to execute sync requests directly between your device and Google's servers.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or note content to any third parties. When using Google Drive synchronization, data transmission occurs directly between your client and Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository or contact us via email at: vesperalabs@gmail.com.
